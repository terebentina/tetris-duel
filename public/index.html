How it works

  • Clear lines to curse your opponent — the more lines at once, the worse it gets:
  • 1 line ☠ their next pieces become awkward S/Z bricks
  • 2 lines 💨 + wind pushes their falling piece sideways
  • 3 lines 🌀 + their piece spins on its own and ⇄ their controls reverse
  • 4 lines 🔥 all of it, longer and harder — plus 🌫 fog hides their stack and a garbage row rises
  • Single player pits you against yourself — every clear curses your own board. How long can you survive your own success?
  • Controls: ←/→ move · ↑/X rotate · Z rotate back · ↓ soft drop · Space hard drop
